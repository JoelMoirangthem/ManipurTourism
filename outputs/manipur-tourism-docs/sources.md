# Source registry

Research cutoff: **20 September 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)**. This registry records what was accessible during desk research, not continuing verification. Recheck dynamic sources before a pitch, integration or launch.

## How to use this registry

- **Primary / accessed:** material published by the responsible organization, with relevant page text inspected. A published statement is not proof of its continued operational accuracy.
- **Secondary / attributed:** a third-party publication, even when it attributes its information to an organizer.
- **Capability evidence:** provider documentation, not an executed integration or language-quality test.
- **Historical:** a policy or previous event edition; do not promote it into current operational information.
- Publication date is **not stated** unless identified below. Retrieval on 20 September does not mean publication or field observation on that date.
- No stakeholder interviews, provider-authenticated API tests, calls to properties, registration submissions, or paid transactions were conducted. Those remain tasks.
- Short quotations and factual summaries are supplied for traceability, not as permission to redistribute entire pages, images or documents.
